# Code of conduct

We want SkeletonStructure to be a welcoming, respectful organization.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, explain clearly, and stay on topic.
- Give credit for others' work.
- Accept feedback and corrections gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to an organization administrator through GitHub. Reports will be handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts when needed to protect the community.
